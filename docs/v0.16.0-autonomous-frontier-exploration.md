# v0.16.0 autonomous frontier exploration

EchoRescue v0.16.0 closes one simulation-only autonomous exploration loop:
take off, construct a local occupancy map from LiDAR, select and fly safe
frontiers, discover and cross a doorway, explore the second room, return over
the final discovered map, LAND, and verify `ON_GROUND` plus disarm.

## Reproduce

Use the pinned environment from the 3D baseline, build the Jazzy workspace, and
source its install tree. Then run:

```bash
./scripts/run_frontier_exploration_integration.sh success --timeout 320 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.0-frontier-headless.json
./scripts/run_frontier_exploration_integration.sh success --graphical --timeout 320 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.0-frontier-graphical.json
./scripts/run_frontier_exploration_integration.sh unreachable --timeout 180 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.0-frontier-unreachable.json
./scripts/run_frontier_exploration_integration.sh sensor_dropout --timeout 180 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.0-frontier-sensor-dropout.json
./scripts/run_frontier_exploration_integration.sh telemetry_reconnect --timeout 200 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.0-frontier-session-recovery.json
./scripts/run_frontier_exploration_integration.sh budget --timeout 220 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.0-frontier-budget.json
```

The harness owns Gazebo, ArduPilot SITL, LiDAR bridge, mission, typed ROS
observer and Gazebo evaluator. Each report includes process termination,
ROS-node disappearance and UDP 9002/14550 plus TCP 5760 availability.

## Contracts

- Map: 34 × 50 cells at 0.25 m, origin `[-2.25, -4.25]` relative to the
  recorded launch ENU pose; interior begins unknown.
- LiDAR: same-session fresh pose association, deterministic rays, monotone
  occupied cells, bounded revisions and stable rejection reasons.
- Planning: cardinal clusters and A* routes through inflated known-free cells
  only; one-metre receding horizon and bounded target/route/replan budgets.
- Completion: no reachable safe frontier after a 1 s quiet interval and at
  least 55% controller-known cells. Gazebo coverage is evaluator-only.
- Reconnect: all prior correlations are invalid; autonomous exploration cannot
  resume. Recovery is bounded and remains a failed mission.

The v0.16.0 world is a versioned derivative of the historical indoor world.
Its small unknown first-room post is moved away from the doorway so the full
0.55 m radius-plus-margin envelope has a valid corridor; the large second-room
blocker, walls and doorway remain unknown to control code.

See [ADR 0024](adr/0024-autonomous-frontier-exploration.md) for the decision and
the JSON artifacts for measured results. Limitations are static obstacles, one
horizontal layer, one simulated drone, no loop closure or occupancy clearing,
and no survivor detection or hardware claim.

## Acceptance results

Both owned-stack success runs passed with no prohibited contact and complete
cleanup. The final headless run produced 33 route generations and 2 replans,
98.82% controller-known cells, 100% evaluator-only sampled coverage in each
room, both doorway crossings, and a 0.204 m final distance from launch. The
graphical run produced 32 generations, 96.29% controller-known cells, 100%
sampled room coverage, both crossings, and a 0.202 m final distance.

The unreachable-frontier, LiDAR-dropout, MAVLink-session-change and target-
budget scenarios all remained failed missions after verified bounded landing
and disarm. Their reports also verify owned-process termination, ROS-node
disappearance, and free UDP 9002/14550 plus TCP 5760 ports.

Real owned-stack regressions were rerun successfully for v0.15.0 known-map
planning, v0.15.1 single-obstacle replanning and v0.15.2 bounded multi-obstacle
replanning. Exact automated validation results are recorded in the final commit
handoff.
