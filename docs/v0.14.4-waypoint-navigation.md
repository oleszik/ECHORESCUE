# v0.14.4 simulation waypoint navigation

## Scope and architecture

v0.14.4 controls one simulated ArduCopter through a fixed, configurable local
3D route. It does not add general planning, obstacle avoidance, an indoor
world, SLAM, dynamic replanning, global/GPS projection, multi-drone flight,
hardware support or real-vehicle support.

The command and observation path is:

```text
Gazebo dynamics -> ArduPilot SITL -> MAVLink telemetry
                                      |
                                      v
                          receive-only bridge hooks
                                      |
                 +--------------------+--------------------+
                 |                                         |
       ROS-independent controller                 typed ROS ENU state
                 |                                         |
       narrow MAVLink command node              independent ROS observer
                 |                                         |
       COMMAND_LONG / local NED       target + event + observed-state checks
```

The original `mavlink_telemetry_bridge` executable remains command-free. The
new `mavlink_waypoint_mission` subclass is the only v0.14.4 flight-control
boundary. Production mission and observer code neither imports simulator APIs
nor reads simulator Ground Truth.

## Mission and coordinate contract

The launch-local ENU pose is recorded from valid, disarmed telemetry in the
active MAVLink session; `(0, 0, 0)` is not assumed. The default relative plan
in `config/waypoint-mission-v0.14.4.json` is:

| ID | East offset | North offset | Altitude above launch |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `waypoint-a` | 3.0 m | 0.0 m | 2.0 m |
| `waypoint-b` | 3.0 m | 3.0 m | 2.5 m |
| `return-launch` | 0.0 m | 0.0 m | 2.0 m |

The MAVLink boundary reuses v0.14.2's ENU-to-NED mapping and sends absolute
`MAV_FRAME_LOCAL_NED` position targets. Optional ENU heading is supported by
the core contract, though the accepted reference plan leaves heading unset.

## Acceptance semantics

`MAV_CMD_SET_MESSAGE_INTERVAL`, `MAV_CMD_DO_SET_MODE`,
`MAV_CMD_COMPONENT_ARM_DISARM`, `MAV_CMD_NAV_TAKEOFF` and `MAV_CMD_NAV_LAND`
must each receive an accepted `COMMAND_ACK` from the issuing session and then
a later telemetry transition. An ACK alone never advances state.

`SET_POSITION_TARGET_LOCAL_NED` is not a MAVLink command-microservice request
and has no `COMMAND_ACK`. Each immutable target is sent once and requires all
of the following:

- successful local serialization/transmission;
- the unchanged active session;
- receipt time and vehicle boot time strictly newer than the transmission
  baselines;
- bounded measurable decrease of total 3D error, unless already arrived;
- simultaneous horizontal and vertical tolerance;
- continuous residence in tolerance for the settling interval.

Default limits are 0.45 m horizontal tolerance, 0.35 m vertical tolerance,
2.0 s settling, 10 s progress timeout, 35 s target timeout, 180 s mission
timeout, an 8 m horizontal geofence, and relative altitude `[-0.25, 4.0]` m.
Targets are geofence-checked before transmission and observed armed state is
checked continuously.

## Failure and recovery

Transmission failure, stale/disconnected telemetry, vehicle-time regression,
no progress, target timeout, geofence violation, unexpected mode, airborne
disarm or unexpected landing stops route advancement. If the vehicle may be
airborne, the v0.14.3 recovery sequencer attempts at most one ACK-and-telemetry-
gated LAND in each MAVLink session.

A reconnect invalidates old ACK, position ordering, target transmission and
completion correlation. A disarmed pre-takeoff reconnect fails closed. An
airborne reconnect never resumes the route: it enters bounded recovery and may
send one new LAND in the new session after fresh armed telemetry. ARM is never
automatically repeated after an uncertain result. Success requires both
`ON_GROUND` and disarmed telemetry after the accepted LAND ACK.

User interruption calls the same bounded recovery path. Owned-stack cleanup
terminates process groups in reverse order and verifies UDP 9002, UDP 14550 and
TCP 5760 can be rebound. Attached mode cleans only mission/observer and records
that pre-existing external processes were preserved.

## Real validation evidence

Both reports were produced headlessly with Gazebo Harmonic 8.11.0, ArduCopter
4.6.3 at `92b0cd788ec29406f26c6f9c31d5ceedbd1cc538`, and ROS 2 Jazzy.

### Owned stack

[`artifacts/v0.14.4-waypoint-smoke-owned.json`](../artifacts/v0.14.4-waypoint-smoke-owned.json)
is `PASS`. The harness started and stopped Gazebo, SITL, mission and observer;
both ROS processes exited 0, all cleanup checks passed, and all three configured
ports were free afterward. One session covered the accepted route.

| Target | Initial error | Settled horizontal | Settled vertical | Time to progress | Time to arrival | Settle |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| waypoint-a | 3.0000 m | 0.0139 m | 0.0100 m | 0.699 s | 2.199 s | 2.000 s |
| waypoint-b | 3.0431 m | 0.0070 m | <0.0001 m | 0.699 s | 2.199 s | 2.100 s |
| return-launch | 4.2730 m | 0.0240 m | <0.0001 m | 0.698 s | 2.599 s | 2.000 s |

Maximum observed altitude was 2.7300 m ENU and maximum launch-relative
horizontal displacement was 4.3198 m. Final landed position was 0.0636 m from
the recorded launch position. Every command ACK had a later telemetry
transition; every target had a strictly later receipt timestamp and vehicle
boot timestamp. LAND ended with `ON_GROUND` and disarmed state. The independent
observer reported the same ordered target and settling events with no session
change.

### Attached stack

[`artifacts/v0.14.4-waypoint-smoke-attached.json`](../artifacts/v0.14.4-waypoint-smoke-attached.json)
is separate `PASS` evidence. The mission and observer exited 0 and were cleaned
up; Gazebo and SITL were explicitly treated as pre-existing and preserved by
the harness. Final launch distance was 0.0575 m. This result demonstrates the
attached mode but is not used as the owned-process lifecycle acceptance test.

## Limitations

- Local targets depend on the ArduPilot EKF startup origin and one stable
  session; reconnect recovery deliberately sacrifices route continuation.
- A position target is transmitted once. This milestone does not stream
  velocity, acceleration, attitude, yaw-rate, RC or raw user-supplied commands.
- The three configured targets are a validation route, not a waypoint upload
  service or path planner.
- Numeric results demonstrate this pinned simulation stack only and are not
  evidence of real-aircraft safety.
