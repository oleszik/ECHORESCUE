# v0.16.1 rendered-camera survivor detection

EchoRescue v0.16.1 adds a simulation-only visual evidence path to autonomous
frontier exploration. A fixed downward-oblique camera supplies actual Gazebo
RGB frames; the controller has no survivor positions or count. Confirmed
reports do not control the exploration completion policy.

## Sensor and geometry contract

The camera publishes `rgb8` images at 320 × 240 with a 60° horizontal field of
view and calibrated intrinsics `fx=fy=277.128`, `cx=160`, `cy=120`. Its optical
frame uses right/down/forward axes and is mounted at body `[0.12, 0, 0.02]` m
with a fixed 60° downward pitch. The camera never gimbals. Projection intersects
the centroid ray with the ENU `up=0` marker plane after applying the fresh
typed vehicle yaw; frames with vehicle roll or pitch outside ±0.35 rad are
rejected rather than approximated.

Gazebo source time, local monotonic receipt time and associated vehicle source
and receipt times remain distinct. Images must have valid `CameraInfo`, match
the dimensions and encoding, increase in sequence and source time, belong to
the current MAVLink session, arrive within 0.5 s, and associate with a vehicle
pose no older than 0.5 s. Processing is capped at 4 Hz with a depth-two
best-effort image queue and a 0.2 s latency budget.

## Controlled marker detector

The versioned rescue marker is a square high-visibility green panel attached to
a static human-shaped prop. Detection converts RGB to HSV, retains hue 35–85,
saturation ≥150 and value ≥100, applies 3 px opening and closing, then extracts
connected components. Components require 80 pixels, at most 25% of the frame,
aspect ratio 0.55–1.8 and fill ≥0.55. Confidence is:

`0.40 × fill + 0.35 × normalized saturation + 0.25 × aspect score`

and must be at least 0.60. A cyan elongated object is the similar-color
distractor. One rescue marker is partially occluded. These rules are designed
only for this controlled simulation and are not a neural or general human
detector.

## Evidence and outcomes

Candidates retain image bounding box and centroid, detector metrics, image and
vehicle times, range, bearing, ENU estimate and deterministic uncertainty.
Confirmation requires at least two distinct increasing frames, mean confidence
≥0.65, compatible estimates within the 1.0 m clustering rule, and either 0.3 s
or 0.2 m viewpoint separation. A session change clears every candidate and
confirmation correlation. IDs such as `survivor-001` are mission-local and do
not encode Gazebo identity.

Controller flight success, perception availability, visual-search completion,
safe landing and evaluator acceptance are reported separately. Camera dropout
allows LiDAR navigation to finish and land safely, but visual-search acceptance
fails explicitly and stale imagery cannot add evidence.

## Reproduce

Use the pinned Gazebo Harmonic, ArduPilot Copter 4.6.3, `ardupilot_gazebo`
revision and ROS 2 Jazzy environment from the 3D baseline, then build and source
the ROS workspace:

```bash
./scripts/run_visual_survivor_integration.sh success --timeout 360 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.1-visual-headless.json
./scripts/run_visual_survivor_integration.sh success --graphical --timeout 360 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.1-visual-graphical.json
./scripts/run_visual_survivor_integration.sh partial_occlusion --timeout 360 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.1-visual-partial-occlusion.json
./scripts/run_visual_survivor_integration.sh distractor --timeout 360 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.1-visual-distractor.json
./scripts/run_visual_survivor_integration.sh camera_dropout --timeout 360 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.1-visual-camera-dropout.json
./scripts/run_visual_survivor_integration.sh session_change --timeout 220 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.1-visual-session-change.json
./scripts/run_visual_survivor_integration.sh budget --timeout 220 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.1-visual-budget.json
./scripts/run_visual_survivor_integration.sh no_survivor --timeout 360 \
  --output artifacts/v0.16.1-visual-no-survivor.json
```

Every owned run terminates Gazebo, SITL and ROS processes and checks UDP
9002/14550, TCP 5760 and visual ROS-node absence. See
[ADR 0025](adr/0025-rendered-camera-survivor-evidence.md) and the compact JSON
artifacts for measured results.

## Limits

Static targets, a controlled color/shape vocabulary, one marker plane, a fixed
camera, planar localization, one simulated vehicle and one indoor world are in
scope. Moving people, learned detection, identity, thermal fusion, semantic
mapping, generalized occlusion reasoning, multi-drone use and hardware are not.
