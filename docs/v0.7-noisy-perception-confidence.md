# v0.7 — Noisy Perception and Confidence

## Motivation and original architecture

v0.7 asks how the existing two-agent SAR strategy behaves when Survivor
perception is uncertain. Previously, `SurvivorSensor` performed range/LOS and
Visual/Smoke or Thermal probability checks. Its confidence controlled each
detection draw, but confirmation was simply “two successful observations”.
False positives were impossible because the adapter iterated only Ground-Truth
Survivor cells.

This slice adds deterministic false negatives, false-positive cell hypotheses,
confidence-weighted evidence, and rejection. Occupancy sensing, mapping,
frontiers, A*, movement, collision avoidance, energy, communication, Relay, and
failure reassignment are unchanged.

## Noise model and deterministic sampling

`--perception-noise off` is the default and retains the exact legacy path. The
`moderate` layer follows range/LOS, channel characteristics, and Smoke:

| Frozen parameter | Visual | Thermal |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| False-negative probability | 0.18 | 0.12 |
| False-positive probability per sweep opportunity | 0.08 | 0.05 |
| False-positive confidence | 0.32–0.52 | 0.32–0.52 |

Decisions hash seed, profile, channel, agent ID, step, observer position, and
target where applicable. Stable sorting and hashes eliminate global randomness
and iteration-order effects.

A false negative suppresses an otherwise successful report. Its hidden target
location remains inside the sensor/evaluator; failed operator telemetry does not
emit it. Each sweep has one background opportunity: a deterministic free,
non-Survivor cell in range with clear LOS. A successful FP draw reports that
cell as ordinary evidence without a Survivor ID or correctness label.

## Confidence, confirmation, and rejection

True-report confidence is the Smoke factor times a linear distance factor
(`1.0` near the observer to `0.75` at maximum range) times channel noise
reliability (`1 − FN rate`). It is conditional on a report, so Thermal's raw
detection probability is not applied twice. False-positive confidence is
sampled from 0.32–0.52. Values stay in `[0, 1]`.

Equal grid cells form one hypothesis. Positive evidence adds `0.5 × confidence`
(cap 1.0). Confirmation requires at least two positives and evidence `>= 0.65`;
normal evidence cannot confirm in one shot. An observable unconfirmed hypothesis
absent from a sweep loses `0.55 × (0.25 × channel raw detection probability)`.
After two negatives, evidence `<= 0.12` rejects it. Confirmed and rejected states
are terminal. No verifier-task strategy was added.

The tracker never receives `GridWorld`. Ground Truth is consulted only at the
sensor boundary and result evaluation. Mission success requires exactly the
true Survivor locations, no false confirmation, zero collisions, and return.

## Telemetry, replay, and dashboard

Evidence events provide channel, range, Smoke, profile, observable raw/final
outcome, confidence, hypothesis cell, evidence before/after, observation count,
and status. Fields that would reveal an unknown missed Survivor are suppressed.
Replay schema 2.1 adds `survivor_hypotheses` to noisy frames; noise-off replay
bytes and legacy schemas are unchanged. The dashboard shows the profile,
unconfirmed cyan rings, rejected red crosses, confirmed yellow markers, and
confidence/evidence/status in event details. It never shows correctness.

## Development set and frozen parameters

Seeds 0–9 were used only for plausibility. An initial draft incorrectly
multiplied Thermal evidence by its raw 0.6 detection probability twice. Making
confidence conditional on a report restored the intended channel relationship.
The tabled rates and thresholds were then frozen before opening seeds 50–99.

## Reliability and failure analysis

Confidence orders correctness monotonically in clear conditions:

| Profile | Buckets 0.2–.4 / .4–.6 / .6–.8 / .8–1 | Samples | Mean confidence | Empirical correctness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B Visual | four populated buckets | 224 / 321 / 1373 / 77 | .360 / .461 / .680 / .820 | 0% / 0% / 100% / 100% |
| D Thermal | four populated buckets | 136 / 211 / 726 / 214 | .359 / .463 / .714 / .823 | 0% / 0% / 100% / 100% |

These bands order evidence but are not calibrated posterior probabilities.
Under Smoke, some correct reports enter low bands (C: 10.4% and 12.53%
correctness; E: 0% and 7.05%).

B misses Survivors at seeds 63, 67, 69, 73, and 89; every case ends safely.
Seeds 57 and 65 create at least 20 hypotheses; seed 60 has a long confirmation
delay. Every noisy holdout seed has an FP observation, but no FP hypothesis is
confirmed. Seed 50 is the demo: it shows FP creation/rejection, confidence
accumulation, confirmation, and safe completion.

## Safety, limitations, and next question

All 250 primary missions have zero wall/drone collisions and all 500 configured
agents return. A vs B duration differs by only 0.28 step due to belief-driven
completion timing, not movement randomness.

Limitations include cell-perfect localization, one background FP opportunity
per sweep, terminal rejected hypotheses, no fusion, and no validated noisy
evidence transport for constrained local knowledge. Confidence is synthetic,
not camera-calibrated. The next separate roadmap question is v0.8 Dynamic
Obstacles and Dynamic Replanning; it is not started here.
